Reading this chart

Saturated air near Boulder and drier air higher up can support a shallow low-cloud setup. These heights are interpolated between sparse HRRR pressure levels. Relative humidity is not cloud-top height. Several markers may share one ~3 km model grid cell.

Before you head up

The index is not a measured thermodynamic inversion, a probability of a beautiful sunrise, or proof the summit will be clear. Check weather alerts, trails, live visibility, and mountain conditions before hiking. Forecast disagreement and unresolved cloud top are meaningful uncertainty.
