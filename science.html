Meteorology and methodology

Temperature inversions, fog/low stratus near Boulder, and clear mountain summits are related but distinct atmospheric events. NOAA HRRR GRIB2 forecast fields are sampled near Boulder and across a foothills transect, using actual issued runs. We combine a surface moisture/stability/wind/visibility/low-cloud setup index with summit-height humidity and middle/high-cloud penalties to estimate scenic favorability.

The indices are hand designed and are not calibrated probabilities. Atmospheric profiles at sparse pressure levels do not resolve the exact top of a shallow fog layer. The HRRR whole-column cloud-top height may refer to upper clouds; altitude-specific moisture is only a proxy for summit visibility.

Historical forecast vintages show how predictions changed as new HRRR cycles arrived. Airport METAR labels are independent observations; they cannot by themselves establish whether the Flatirons were above a cloud deck. Previous ML research code is kept in the archive, not used in active forecasts.
