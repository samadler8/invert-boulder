Using the experimental predictor

Run the Python server from the README, open Dashboard, and inspect the morning scores for each day. The two separate XGBoost and CNN estimates are shown in the verdict panel. Their average is the large score.

A proxy-trained score is NOT a proven probability of an inversion, a sea of clouds, or successful summit visibility.

Click a day and a bar to inspect its forecast hour. The second chart mode shows forecast low cloud cover. Time labels follow Boulder local time including the DST transition.

The preset buttons create entirely synthetic scenarios to demonstrate the chart and schematic. They do not represent weather forecasts. Only the Live button requests forecast data.

In live mode the diagram will not invent a cloud-top altitude or promise that a landmark is above fog. Check actual webcams, local weather observations, wind and trail conditions before hiking.

If the dashboard shows Models not available, train and save the models first (see repository README). A network connection is needed to fetch GFS forecasts.
