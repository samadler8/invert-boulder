What is this Dashboard?

The Boulder Inversion Tracker is a highly specialized meteorological tool designed for photographers, hikers, and weather enthusiasts in Boulder, Colorado. It predicts the exact probability and elevation of temperature inversions—the phenomenon that traps the city in a dense fog deck while upper elevations sit under clear blue skies[cite: 3].

The dashboard uses a 3-level elevation profile comparing the City of Boulder (5,400 ft), the 1st Flatiron (7,200 ft), and the Green Mountain Summit (8,100 ft) to determine which locations will be above the clouds[cite: 3].

Understanding the Terminology (Presets)

  • Live Forecast: Pulls real-time atmospheric sounding data to give you the current 7-day outlook[cite: 3].
  • Classic Inversion: The "holy grail" setup. The cloud ceiling stops around 6,700 ft. The city is foggy, but if you hike to the 1st Flatiron or Green Mountain, you will break through the clouds into the sunshine[cite: 3].
  • High Deck: A deeper moisture layer. The fog ceiling rises to around 7,500 ft. The 1st Flatiron is stuck in the fog, and only the very top of Green Mountain sits above the clouds[cite: 3].
  • Socked In: The moisture layer is very deep (above 8,100 ft). All three landmarks (City, Flatiron, Green Mountain) are trapped inside the cloud layer[cite: 3].
  • Bluebird / Downslope: No inversion exists. The air is either very dry, or strong westerly winds (Chinooks) have blown the moisture away, resulting in clear air everywhere[cite: 3].

How to Use the Interactive Charts

1. The 3D Elevation Cross-Section

This diagram visually maps the estimated cloud top against Boulder's terrain. It features dynamic tags that tell you whether each of the 3 landmarks is currently ☁️ IN FOG or ☀️ ABOVE CLOUDS[cite: 3]. When inversion probability drops below 20%, the fog disappears and a yellow "Clear Mountain Air" banner displays[cite: 3].

2. The 7-Day Interactive Strip

Click on any day in the 7-day strip. Doing so will recalculate the entire page for that specific day's peak inversion window, updating the gauge, metrics, and diagrams[cite: 3].

3. The 24-Hour Diurnal Timeline

Located below the 7-day outlook, this chart lets you scrub through the day hour-by-hour. You can toggle between viewing the Inversion Probability (%) and the estimated Cloud Top Elevation (ft)[cite: 3]. Click on any bar in this chart, and the 3D Elevation Cross-Section above will instantly animate to show the conditions for that exact hour[cite: 3].
