Reading the forecast

The scenic and low-cloud indices are **not probabilities**. Scenic suitability looks for low cloud over Boulder, drying before summit elevation, and limited middle/high clouds. HRRR's total cloud top may represent upper-level clouds, and an RH drying transition is not a measured fog top.

The time slider changes only among forecasts you have actually downloaded. The mountain cross-section is illustrative. Multiple map locations can share HRRR's ~3 km grid cells.

Forecast history

Compare T−24, T−18, T−12, T−6 and T−0. T−0 is a model forecast available near the event time, not an observation. Captured predictions are distinct from later reconstructions. KBDU METAR reports are airport observations, not summit observations.

Refresh

Open the website and click Refresh everything, or run python -m scripts.quicklook --refresh-only. Historical downloads take longer on first retrieval. The XGBoost/CNN research pipeline is archived and not used by this website.
