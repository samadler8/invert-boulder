Understanding the short-range tracker

The live dashboard shows estimates for airport low-cloud ceiling conditions from an initialized HRRR run. Forecast lead times are strictly less than 40 hours from model initialization. Because model results are not distributed instantaneously, the remaining future window may be shorter than 40 hours.

Forecast probabilities are experimental and only valid to the extent independently observed verification supports them. The first model uses reports from Boulder Municipal Airport (KBDU), not photos from the Flatirons.

Reading the probability and charts

The large percentage is the average of an XGBoost model and a small CNN estimating a conservatively defined reported low-ceiling event. The models receive numerical weather predictions of surface humidity, visibility, cloud cover, winds and vertical structure. Click a day or an hourly bar to inspect a specific forecast valid time. The other chart mode shows predicted low-cloud coverage, not a measured cloud-top height.

Elevation-specific outlook

Each landmark is shown at its target elevation. Elevation-conditioned scenic training is not enabled without verified summit data. Otherwise it says Not trained. If enough reported low cloud bases exist, a separate regression model estimates the airport low-cloud base altitude, not the cloud-top or safe breakout elevation.

Do not use airport fog probability as a guarantee that Boulder is covered while Green Mountain is sunny. The diagram is schematic and does not indicate observed summit clearance.

Synthetic presets

Classic, high, socked and clear presets are illustrative examples only. Their numbers and cloud layers are invented to test the interface. The live mode never replaces a failed data request with a synthetic prediction.

For contributors

See the README for collecting independent airport METAR observations, retrieving issue-time-preserved HRRR runs, training the models, adding summit observations, and running the local FastAPI server. Current provider data may be sparse for rare fog cases. The model refuses to train when it cannot validate both classes.
